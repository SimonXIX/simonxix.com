## *TAKE ONE Presents...*

*TAKE ONE Presents...* is a podcast series critically analysing blockbuster film franchies that I produce and co-host with [Jim Ross](https://bsky.app/profile/jimgr.bsky.social) as part of [*TAKE ONE*](https://takeonecinema.net/). Subscribe at [https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/takeonepresents](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/takeonepresents) and check out references for each episode in [our Zotero library](https://www.zotero.org/groups/5642177/take_one/library).

Over the three series of the podcast so far, we have covered the *Alien* franchise on [*The Xenopod*](https://takeonecinema.net/thexenopod/), the *Jurassic Park* franchise on [*The Dinopod*](https://takeonecinema.net/the-dinopod/), the *Mission: Impossible* franchise on [*The Impossipod*](https://takeonecinema.net/the-impossipod/), and are currently analysing the *Terminator* franchise on [*Pod With Us If You Want To Live*](https://takeonecinema.net/podwithusifyouwanttolive/).
